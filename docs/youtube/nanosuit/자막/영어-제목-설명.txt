Can We Really Build Iron Man's Nanosuit? Nanorobots, Exoskeletons and Jet Suits Explained

[긴 영상 - 영어 설명]
Could Iron Man's nanosuit ever be real? We split the movie suit into five technologies - self-assembling robots, shape-changing materials, self-healing materials, wearable exoskeletons and jet suits - and look at how far real labs and companies have come.
Bottom line: the whole suit is still far away, but its pieces are already growing.
