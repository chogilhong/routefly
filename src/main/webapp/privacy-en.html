routefly Privacy Policy

Effective: October 21, 2026 · This is an English translation for convenience. If it differs from the Korean original (개인정보처리방침), the Korean original prevails.

routefly has no sign-up or login and does not collect personal information such as names or contact details (except an email address you choose to enter in Feedback to get a reply, and a sender name you choose to enter in Sending a course). Your GPS location is used only on your own phone and is not sent to our server, and your hike records and photos are stored only on your phone. The only things kept on the server are access logs (IP address etc.), which are deleted automatically after a set period.

1. Information the server receives and how it is used

Access logsIP address, time of access, path of the requested page or data (search words and map areas after "?" in the address are not kept), browser type. Created automatically when you connect.
Used for: providing the service, blocking hacking and excessive requests, checking failures
"Near me"Your location is not sent. Your phone receives the course lists of a few fixed map cells that divide Korea into squares of about 10 km (the same for every user), then picks courses within 5 km of you and measures distances on the phone. To the server, the request looks the same as moving the map to those cells.
Request countingTo block too many requests in a short time, request counts per IP address are kept briefly in server memory only (deleted after 2 minutes without requests; never written to files or the database).

Course search words, course numbers and map areas are used only to find lists and are not stored.

Location information

routefly uses your phone's location (GPS) only on that phone for the map, remaining distance, off-course alerts and records, and does not send it to or store it on the operator's server. The operator therefore cannot know where you are. Your location goes elsewhere only when you choose it yourself (directions, SOS text and sharing in section 4 below) and when you join a hiking club room below and agree.

Hiking club rooms (from October 20, 2026)

A guided hiking club leader creates a room before the hike, and members who booked join with the room code. Only the location of people who agreed when joining is shown to that room's leaders (including company staff the leader gave a leader code to). Members do not see each other's locations.

routefly has filed a location-based service business report under the Location Information Act (Korea Media and Communications Commission, report No. 1857; business: location-based hiking information service for users). Location-Based Service Terms

What we receiveRoom: hike name, departure and descent deadline times, leader name and contact (as the leader enters them), number of buses and plate numbers, number of seats, pickup stops. If the room is linked with a company code, that company office also sees this room information and assistant leader names (2026-10-28).
Return pickup point (2026-10-28): where to board the bus after the descent (e.g. Seoraksan C district 17:00) - shown to leaders and the company office, deleted when the room ends.
Drop-off point (2026-10-28): where a member or leader chose to get off (e.g. Hangyeryeong, Osaek, waiting on the bus) - shown to leaders and the company office, deleted when the room ends.
Booking list (2026-10-24): only member names and seat numbers pasted by the leader (no contact details). Until that member joins there is no location, and the leader's screen just shows "no phone".
People who joined: name (nickname), role (hiker or leader), seat number and pickup stop, departure and return boarding marks, location (latitude, longitude, accuracy), battery %, SOS (every 30 seconds from your stop's departure time or from when you board), and the path a hiking member has walked since departure (one point every 10 m of movement). While you follow a trail in the hiking screen, also its estimated finish time and end point (return ETA - shown to the leader and the company office as "ETA · late for the bus", kept in server memory only, 2026-11-11). When no location can be found (indoors, deep valleys), only the "phone is on" time, battery and SOS are sent, without location.
Once a member joins by link, QR or NFC, the top line of the hike screen shows "Sharing your location with the leader · purpose: hiking safety · last shared time" (immediate notice of each third-party provision - Location Information Act Article 19(3)). Browsers send only while that screen is on. If you join with the routefly Android app, it keeps sending even with the screen off, from the departure time of your pickup stop (or when you board the bus) until you board for the return (or the hike is cancelled or the room ends), and a "routefly - hiking club room" notification stays in the notification bar during that time.
A one-line message (up to 80 characters) a member sends with "💬 To leader" is seen only by that room's leaders, kept only in server memory (never written to files or logs) and deleted when the room ends.
Who sees itOnly the leaders (the chief leader who created the room and assistant leaders who joined with the assistant leader code - as many as there are buses) and, if the chief leader linked the room with a company code, that company office (members' screens show everyone who receives their location): name, seat, SOS, return boarding, location, battery, pickup stop, departure boarding, time spent in one place, and the last 2 hours of the path of a member tapped in the list (in case of distress). Before joining, only the room introduction (name, departure, leader, head count, taken seat numbers). There is no screen that shows this to the operator.
Where and how longWalked paths are kept only in server memory (never written to files or the database; erased when the server restarts) and deleted when you leave the room or the room ends. Each person's last location, battery and SOS are also kept in the same server file as the room information (readable only by that server account, mode 600) so a person in distress is not lost when the server restarts (deploys, maintenance) (2026-11-02), and are deleted together when you stop sharing, leave the room or the room ends. Room information (name, times, leader name and contact, where the bus is parked) and nicknames, seats, pickup stops and boarding marks are kept in a server file so they last until the hike day. Your own entry is deleted when you leave the room, and the whole room is deleted when the leader ends the room or cancels the hike, or 6 hours after the descent deadline (24 hours if any member turned on SOS or, after the hike started, is not yet confirmed down, in case of distress; at most 40 days after creation). Right after a server restart a room gets one more hour so members are not cut off, but never more than 3 hours past that time (2026-11-03). If the room information file cannot be read, it is kept as a mode-600 copy (party.json.bad-<time> etc.) for inspection and deleted after 7 days (2026-11-03).
Anonymous course statisticsWhen a room ends (except cancelled hikes), the paths walked by hiking members are made unidentifiable and used for course statistics (actual time per section, popular paths, correcting estimated times). They are stored in the database without nicknames, keys, room codes or times (only the hike date and seconds since the first point), excluding the first and last 500 m and sections ridden in a vehicle. Paths of people who left the room partway are not kept. As the data cannot identify anyone, there is no separate deletion period.
Records required by lawAs records confirming the use and provision of location information, only the times of joining, removal, not boarding at departure, room end and SOS, with the room code and in-room number, are kept (no coordinates or nicknames), for more than 6 months (200 days) and then deleted. When a member withdraws consent (stop sharing, leaving the room), that member's records are deleted immediately (Location Information Act Article 24(4)).

Children under 14 cannot join (the Location Information Act requires a legal guardian's consent, which we do not collect). You can stop at any time with "Stop sharing" at the top of the hike screen (only your name stays in the leader's list). To stop sending your location, tap Stop sharing or turn off location permission.

Feedback (from October 20, 2026)

We receive the text you write in "📮 Feedback", the email address for a reply only if you enter one, and which screen you came from (address path). Location, device information and IP address are not stored with it (to block sending too often, only counts per IP address are kept in server memory for one day).

Received messages are sent to the operator's email (Gmail) and also kept in a server file. They are used only for replies and improving the service, and deleted after 1 year (12 months).

Sending a course (from November 1, 2026)

Only when you tap 📐 GPX editor → "📮 Submit course" (from November 2, 2026 - a 📒 My records entry is sent by opening it with 🗺 View record → 📐 Edit GPX, from November 24, 2026) and agree, we receive the selected track's line shape (point positions and elevation - no times, without the start and end parts you trimmed), the course name, the sender name exactly as you typed it (only if you enter one) and a note. Photos and photo notes are not sent.

It is used only so the operator can review it and publish it as a public course. If published, the sender name is shown as "Made by" on the course screens and mountain pages (visible to everyone - it is not added to the course name). It is kept in a server file and deleted right after the review, and at the latest after 30 days. It is not linked to other information such as your IP address (to block sending too often, only counts per IP address are kept in server memory for one day).

Public record links (from November 22, 2026)

Only when you tap 📒 My records → 📈 → 🎬 For video → "🔗 Make a public record link" and confirm, the operator's server stores that record's route shape (point positions and heights - no times, always without the first and last 300 m) and its name, date, distance, time taken and climb in a file. Photos and notes are not uploaded. Anyone with the link (record.html?id=…) can see it; it is not shown in search engines.

New links cannot be made since November 22, 2026 (the make and turn-off buttons were removed; on November 24 the server's make entry was closed too); existing links can only be viewed. They are deleted automatically one year after they were made. To delete one sooner, send the link address to the contact in section 9. The delete key left on the device that made it (rf-pub) is no longer used. It is not linked to other information such as your IP address.

🎣 tidefly (fishing trips) (from November 15, 2026)

When you tap 📍 Near me, your location is used only on this device to pick the nearest spots and is not sent to the server. Lodging and food nearby are looked up by our server with Korea Tourism Organization and Kakao using the fishing spot's coordinates, not your location. The spot search text is filtered on the screen only. If what you type in the trail-list search is not a mountain name, it is sent to our server only to check whether it is a fishing spot or fish name, and is not stored. Alerts you choose (leave the rocks, before sunset) ring on the device while this page is open; in the app they are also set as phone notifications so they ring with the screen closed (from November 18, 2026, not sent to the server). A file you pick with "⬆ Load a CSV" in the catch log is also read only on this device.

dronefly (drones) (from November 17, 2026)

When you tap "My location", your location is used only on this device to compare with the airspace file (V-World aviation layers) and is not sent to the server. Only the place name you type in the search is sent to the operator's server, which asks Kakao · V-World for its coordinates and does not store it. Heritage protection zones are looked up by the operator's server from V-World by the fixed grid cell of about 10 km around the spot (the same cell for every user), not by your location. Flying weather is also looked up by the operator's server from the Korea Meteorological Administration with the centre of the fixed cell of about 5 km around the spot (the same cell for every user), not your location (from November 18, 2026). The flight-approval summary is made on the screen only; you copy it into Drone One-stop yourself. In the app, "🔔 Flight-day morning alert" sets that date, time and place name only as a phone notification. A file you pick with "⬆ Load a CSV" in the flight log is also read only on this device (from November 18, 2026, not sent to the server).

2. Information stored only on your phone

The following is kept only in this device's browser (app) storage and is not sent to the server. The operator cannot see it either.

  • GPS location and walked path (shown on screen during the hike, used for remaining distance and saved records)
  • Hike records (latest 30), photos and notes taken during hikes, GPX files you opened
  • 🛩 3D flight videos are made on this device and kept only briefly while it is handed back to the follow screen (rf-video · rf-fly-rec). The key to delete a public record link (rf-pub)
  • When a GPX file opened with 📂 Open GPX in the record view is passed to 📐 Edit GPX, its line and name are kept briefly (rf-studio-in, deleted as soon as the editor takes it - from November 24, 2026)
  • Weight and height for calorie and step estimates (if entered), certification center and 100 famous mountains summit stamps, completion marks, saved courses, last search words
  • Courses and map images saved for offline use
  • routefly display mode (light · dark, rf-theme), the follow map base (satellite · light map, rf-basemap) and contour lines on · off (rf-contour)
  • tidefly alert choices, opened regions, display mode, text size, language, favourite spots, catch log and its photos, and the day you last saved the catch log as CSV (tf-alarms · tf-open-areas · tf-theme · tf-size · tf-lang · tf-favs · tf-catch · tf-catch-saved · photos in tf-photos)
  • dronefly drone max wind, today's pre-flight checklist, flight log, and the written-test questions you got wrong, where to continue, your scores and the last test you picked, and the day you last saved the flight log as CSV (df-wind · df-check · df-log · df-quiz · df-quiz-at · df-quiz-score · df-quiz-set · df-log-saved)
  • Tracks open in the GPX editor and your edits (restored when you come back - removed when you take them all off the list)
  • Settings and recent items for Handy tools, games and mountain pages (exchange rate, fuel and tool settings, best game scores, recently viewed mountains), your hiking club nickname and company office keys, the sender name for sending a course, and the name on completion certificates

How to delete: delete records in "📒 Records" on the hike screen, or delete site data in your browser settings (app: App info → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall). The app does not include this information in phone backups. To keep your records, download a backup file (records, photos, stamps, saved courses) with "📒 Records → 💾 Save backup", or save each record with "Save GPX". The backup file is made on your phone and sent only where you choose (drive, email etc.), never to our server.

4. Information given to others

  • The operator does not give or sell personal information to others (except when investigative agencies etc. request it under the law).
  • Map images: your browser fetches satellite photos and map labels directly from the Ministry of Land's V-World servers and terrain heights from AWS (Terrain Tiles) servers. The map area you are viewing and your IP address reach those servers.
  • Only when you choose: tapping directions (Kakao Map, Naver Map, Google Maps) passes the start point and your location to that app; SOS texts and location photos, course links and record sharing go to the app you choose (text, messenger etc.). After that, that service's policy applies.
  • Weather, fine dust, hiking safety, lodging, restaurants and pet information: the operator's server asks the Korea Meteorological Administration, Korea Environment Corporation (AirKorea), Korea Forest Service, National Medical Center and Korea Tourism Organization using the coordinates of the course trailhead (the summit on mountain pages). To build the link to the KMA weather site, the same coordinates are used to ask Kakao for the neighborhood (administrative dong) name and code. The "🍜 Food" link after a hike asks only for the neighborhood name of the course end point (without a course, the center of the fixed cell of about 5 km where you finished) and searches that neighborhood; the same coordinates are used to ask Kakao for places within 30 km under TV show names (such as Baekban Gihaeng) shown as "📺 TV-featured food" (from November 22, 2026, not stored - the server remembers the answer for the same spot for a day). On a phone, tapping it hands the spot where you finished to the Kakao Map app on your phone to search around it (not sent to our server; without the app, the neighborhood search above). When you tap "Use my location" in Tools, the center of the fixed cell of about 5 km around you (the same for everyone in that cell) is used instead of your location. The rain-radar check every 10 minutes while recording a hike uses the same fixed cell of about 5 km center. The weather-warning and wildfire-risk re-check every 90 minutes while recording uses the same cell center. The record viewer (📂 Open GPX) sends only the national park name when a record lies in a national park (decided on the phone from the park shelters and park-named mountains it passes) to get the park trail lines - the record's positions and file are not sent (from November 23, 2026). Your location and IP address are not passed on.
  • Only when you use that tool, your browser asks these services directly (not through the operator's server - they see your IP address): the Handy tools "postcode · English address" sends the address you type to the Kakao (Daum) postcode service, "exchange rates" asks Frankfurter (a public European Central Bank rate service on overseas servers) with the base currency only, and the GPX editor's "draw a new path" sends only the map area around the two points you tapped to public OpenStreetMap Overpass servers (Germany and other countries) when the operator's server has no path there.
  • Affiliate links: tapping a Coupang Partners, Agoda, Trip.com, SOCAR, HotelsCombined or Airalo (LinkPrice) affiliate link takes you to that company's site, where its cookies and privacy policy apply. The operator earns a commission when you buy or book, but no personal information such as who bought what reaches the operator.

6. Cookies and visit analysis

We do not use login cookies or external analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) (for ads, see section 10). The information in section 2 is kept in this device's browser storage, not in cookies. (Only the operator's own devices get a cookie marking "don't count operator visits". routefly creates no cookies for visitors; on pages that show ads, Google may set ad cookies - see section 10.)

To improve the service, the operator counts daily visits and visitors per screen and mountain page as numbers only. Visitors are de-duplicated by mixing the IP address with a value used only that day (discarded the next day), and no IP addresses or identifying information are kept in the statistics. The kinds of access that day (people, search robots, scraping programs etc.) and the most opened pages are viewed only in server memory on the operator's statistics screen (addresses as a same-day hash, browser type text), never written to files and deleted when the day changes (2026-10-29 - to detect and block scraping programs). Which site (Naver, Google etc.) visitors came through is counted only by site name, without referring addresses or search words.

7. Your rights · requesting data deletion

There is no sign-up, so almost no personal information stays on the server for long. Group room data (nickname, name, contact, location) and course submissions (sender name) are also deleted automatically within the periods in section 3. You can turn off location permission in your phone settings at any time; without it you can only view courses and use test walks.

How to request deletion of routefly (app and web) data (written this way from 17 November 2026):

  1. Group room: tap "Stop sending" on the "📡 … leader" line at the top of the hike screen, or "Leave room" in the room screen. Your location is deleted at once, and leaving deletes everything of yours in that room (nickname, location, confirmation records).
  2. Information on your phone (hike records, photos, notes, GPX editor): delete it yourself as described in section 2 (in the app: App info → Storage → Clear data).
  3. Other server data (access logs, feedback, course submissions, the "made by" name on a published course): email the contact in section 9 with "routefly data deletion request" and what to delete (date, room, course name, etc.). We delete it without delay after checking and reply by email.

The data deleted and the retention periods are as in the table in section 3. No data is kept longer for legal reasons.

8. How we keep it safe

  • All connections are encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Servers and the database get only the permissions they need (the web app only reads course data), and the database cannot be reached directly from outside.
  • We block excessive requests and do not show internal information on error screens.

10. Changes

Ads (2026-10-28; tools and stamps pages added 2026-11-01): Google AdSense ads may appear on mountain and course introduction pages, on the tools and stamps (completions) pages, and at the sides of the trails page and the follow screen's course list on wide PC screens (from 2026-11-24) - not on the follow map once a course is open, SOS or hiking club room screens, nor in the app or at the sides on phones; auto ads are not used. Google and other ad companies may use cookies to show ads based on this browser's visits to this and other sites; routefly does not receive the contents of those cookies. You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ad Settings; how Google uses cookies in advertising is explained in Google's advertising policy.

Moving records between devices (2026-10-28, 📒 Records → QR): the sending device encrypts the record backup on the device and leaves it on the server in pieces; it is deleted as soon as the receiving device takes it (or after 10 minutes if not taken; memory only). With a QR code, the key is only inside the QR and never reaches the server. With an 8-character code, the key is made from the code, so it can only be opened by someone who learns the code within 10 minutes (the server never receives the code). Use QR codes and codes only between your own devices and do not send them to others.

Finding lodging near a place (2026-10-29, "🏨 Find lodging near a place" on the course list /m/): the place name you type (e.g. Sancheong County Office) is used by the operator's server only to ask Kakao (local search) and the Ministry of Land's V-World for its coordinates, and those coordinates are used to ask Kakao (lodging category search) and the Korea Tourism Organization for lodging nearby. Your location is not used. Lodging photos (Korea Tourism Organization) and the small map images (V-World) are loaded by your browser directly from those servers, so they can see your connection address (IP). Names and coordinates are kept in server memory for one day to avoid repeated queries, then deleted, and are not written to files or access logs (which drop everything after "?"). Tapping a booking button takes you to that site (affiliate link), where its policy applies.

If this policy changes, we announce it on this screen from 7 days before it takes effect.
